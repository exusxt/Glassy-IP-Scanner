What it does

Everything you need to see your network

From a single scan to continuous monitoring — one app covers the whole picture.

Fast scanning

Scan single IPs, ranges or CIDR blocks with configurable speed, pause/resume/cancel and real-time results.

Device intelligence

Hostnames, MAC vendors and device-type classification — plus custom names, notes, tags and favorites.

Port scanner

Built-in TCP port scanning with presets, custom ranges and service names for well-known ports.

Network map

Interactive topology with the gateway at the center, SNMP switch detection, zoom/pan and image export.

Monitoring

A persistent known-device ledger raises new-device, back-online and went-offline alerts between scans.

Scan history

Every scan is recorded; compare any two to see exactly what changed on your network.

Export results

Write any scan's results — including known-but-offline devices — to CSV or JSON for further analysis.

Wake-on-LAN

Power on offline devices that have a known MAC from their detail dialog — a single magic packet on the LAN.

Offline persistence

Known devices that go offline stay visible with their saved metadata instead of vanishing from the results.

Smart classification

Routers, NAS, consoles, phones, printers and smart devices detected from OUI vendors and hostname rules.

Backup & restore

Export all local data — settings, profiles, map, ledger, history — to a single JSON file and move between machines.

Privacy first

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. All scanning and data storage happens on your machine.

Three steps

How it works

Pick a target, scan, and explore — everything happens on your machine.

1 — Choose targets

Enter a single IP, a range like 192.168.1.10–192.168.1.50, or a CIDR block like 192.168.1.0/24, and tune the scan speed.

2 — Scan & probe

Devices respond to ICMP echo, ARP discovery and TCP probes. Results stream in live, with latency and open ports.

3 — Understand & act

Dig into each device's detail dialog, explore the network map, export the results, or keep watching with monitoring.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

No. Everything — scanning, the network map, the monitoring ledger and scan history — runs locally on your machine. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.
It combines ICMP echo (ping), ARP discovery for local MAC addresses and TCP probes for ports. A device counts as online only via a real probe response — stale ARP entries are never treated as liveness.
Hosts that ignore ping show up via ARP but without a hostname, and some vendors' MAC OUIs aren't in the built-in database. You can always rename and tag them yourself, and the classification learns from common hostname patterns.
A persistent ledger remembers every device the scanner has ever seen. Across scans it raises alerts when a device is new, comes back online, or goes offline — so changes on your network never go unnoticed.
Basic ICMP and TCP scanning works without elevation. ARP discovery and some OS-level probes on Windows and Linux may require administrator/root rights — the app tells you when it needs them.
